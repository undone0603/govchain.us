How It Works

Verification in Four Steps

GovChain wraps your existing QR code and inventory workflows with cryptographic proof — without disrupting your operations.

1

Register Product

Assign a unique GovChain identity to each item at the point of manufacture or contract award.

2

Anchor to Chain

Product metadata and custody events are hashed and written to an immutable blockchain ledger.

3

Scan QR Code

Anyone in the supply chain scans the product's QR code with any smartphone camera.

4

Instant Proof

Live verification result with full audit trail available to inspectors, auditors, and contracting officers in under 2 seconds.
